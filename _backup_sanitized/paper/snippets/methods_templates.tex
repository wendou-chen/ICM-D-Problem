\subsection{Heuristic Optimization Framework}
In this study, we employ a hybrid heuristic framework combining Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), Genetic Algorithm (GA), and Simulated Annealing (SA) to navigate the complex landscape of the proposed optimization problem.

\subsubsection{Algorithm Selection}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO):} Utilized for global exploration in the continuous relaxation of the solution space. PSO effectively identifies promising regions by mimicking social behavior.
    \item \textbf{Genetic Algorithm (GA):} Employed for structural learning and discrete optimization. Through crossover and mutation operators, GA refines binary/integer decisions.
    \item \textbf{Simulated Annealing (SA):} Applied as a local search mechanism to escape local optima by accepting non-improving moves with a probability decreasing over temperature $T$.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Exploration vs. Exploitation}
The proposed pipeline balances exploration (PSO) and exploitation (SA). The early phase focuses on diversity maintenance to avoid premature convergence, while the late phase intensifies search around the incumbent solution.
