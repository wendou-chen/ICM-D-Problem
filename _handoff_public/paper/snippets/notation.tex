\caption{Notations and Definitions}
\begin{tabular}{c|l}
\hline
\textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Description} \\ \hline
$G(V,E)$ & The directed graph representing the network infrastructure. \\
$N$ & Set of nodes, $|N| = n$. \\
$E$ & Set of edges, $|E| = m$. \\
$x_{ij}$ & Binary decision variable, 1 if edge $(i,j)$ is selected, 0 otherwise. \\
$c_{ij}$ & Cost associated with traversing edge $(i,j)$. \\
$w_{ij}$ & Weight or capacity of edge $(i,j)$. \\
$\mathcal{P}$ & Set of available paths for OD pairs. \\
$f_k$ & Flow volume for commodity $k$. \\
$T$ & Temperature parameter in Simulated Annealing. \\
$\alpha$ & Cooling rate for annealing schedule. \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\label{tab:notation}
